Backing up

A backup is a small file with your whole list. If this app is ever cleared or you get a new phone, the file brings everything back.

Where I save backups

The directions below use this location.

To back up

  1. Tap Back Up Now.
  2. When the share menu appears, tap Save to Files.
  3. Go to and tap Save.

To restore

  1. Tap Restore from Backup below.
  2. In the file picker, go to .
  3. Pick the newest file. Each one is named with its date.
  4. Confirm that you want to replace your current list.

If the app itself is gone

Open the app's web address in Safari once with a connection, add it to your Home Screen again, then restore your backup as above. Restoring works with no connection.

Back up your changes?

Your latest change is only saved on this phone until you back up.
